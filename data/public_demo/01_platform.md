# Northstar Platform Overview

Northstar is a fictional enterprise knowledge platform used for this public RAG benchmark. Its tenant-safe document service is named Atlas. Atlas accepts PDF, Markdown, and plain-text sources. The platform release described here is Aurora 3.2, published on 14 March 2026. Northstar's default semantic index uses cosine similarity, and the public demo stores vectors locally in FAISS. The platform team owns ingestion and retrieval reliability.
